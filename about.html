About StudentHub

StudentHub is a comprehensive semester project engineered under ITUE203: Web Development Frameworks at CHARUSAT, Faculty of Technology & Engineering.

Academic Context & Objectives

The objective of this portal is to transition students from raw HTML/CSS fundamentals into production-ready web application development with full-stack capabilities, security hardening, database integration, and modern asynchronous frontend paradigms.

Course Code: ITUE203
Semester: 3rd Semester, 2026-27 (Odd)
Departments: CSPIT (CE/IT/CSE/AIML), DEPSTAR (CE/CSE/IT)
University: CHARUSAT, Changa
